Overview

Generative search and LLM platforms like Google AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, and emerging ChatGPT Search are reshaping how consumers discover financial products. As search shifts from links to conversational answers, paid advertising models, measurement frameworks, and compliance expectations are evolving rapidly, especially for highly regulated BFS brands.

In this on demand webinar, we've explored the current and emerging paid advertising opportunities across generative search and AI-driven platforms, what's realistically available to BFS marketers today, and how to prepare for what's coming next.
