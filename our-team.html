Vish Sastry Rachakonda

Founder & Executive Chairman

Vish Sastry Rachakonda

VP, Search & Site Experience

Sastry founded iQuanti in 2008 and as CEO has grown it into a trusted performance marketing partner for some of the world's leading brands, particularly in banking and financial services. Now as Executive Chairman (and Chief Cheerleader!) Sastry continues to champion iQuanti's mission of driving superior digital performance through data-led capabilities. He is a zealous guardian of the company's values, and remains actively engaged in shaping strategic direction and culture.

Before starting iQuanti, Sastry worked at Discover and American Express, where he built deep expertise in marketing. He lives with his wife Meena and their dog Bruno in Jersey City.
